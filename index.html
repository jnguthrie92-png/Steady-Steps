Privacy Policy for Steady Steps

Steady Steps ("the app," "we," "us") is a step-tracking app for Android. This policy explains what information the app collects, what it doesn't, and how the third-party services it uses handle data.

The short version

Your step count, height, weight, and goal settings are stored only on your own phone. We don't collect them, see them, or send them anywhere. The only data leaving your device is what Google's advertising service collects to show ads, exactly as it would in any other free, ad-supported app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following directly on your phone, using Android's standard local app storage:

  • Your daily step counts and history
  • Your height, weight, and daily step goal

None of this is transmitted to us or to any server we control. We have no account system, no sign-in, and no way to see this information ourselves. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted from your device along with it.

Permissions the app requests, and why

PermissionWhy it's needed
Physical activity (step sensor)To read your phone's step-counter sensor and calculate your daily steps, distance, and calories.
NotificationsTo show the ongoing step-count notification while tracking runs in the background.
Run in the background / start on bootSo step counting continues after you close the app or restart your phone, rather than only while the app is open.
Battery optimization exemptionOptional, and only used if you tap the in-app button for it — helps background tracking survive your phone's power-saving features.
Internet accessRequired by the advertising service (see below) to fetch and report on ads. The app itself does not use the internet for anything else.

Advertising

Steady Steps is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect information such as your advertising ID, general device information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us — we don't receive or store it.

You can review Google's own privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy, and you can limit ad personalization at any time in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by Android version and manufacturer).

What we don't do

  • We don't require or collect your name, email address, or any account information
  • We don't track your real-time location
  • We don't sell any data — we don't have any of your data to sell
  • We don't share your step, height, weight, or goal data with anyone, because it never leaves your device

Children's privacy

Steady Steps is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
